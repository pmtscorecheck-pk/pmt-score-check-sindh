PMT Score Check in Sindh: How Your Score Decides Eligibility in Sindh

Last updated: 7 November 2026

In Sindh, a PMT Score Check works the same way as the rest of Pakistan — through the 8171 web portal or by SMS to 8171 — but the schemes it unlocks and the offices you visit are provincial. Checking first takes two minutes and can save a wasted trip to an office that cannot help you until the record is right.

What the check decides in Sindh

The score behind the check comes from the household survey record — family size, income, housing, education and work. In Sindh that record decides eligibility for BISP cash support and for provincial support that uses the same score. A lower score generally points to greater need, and each scheme sets its own line. The check does not change the score; it only shows what the record currently says.

How to do a PMT Score Check by CNIC

There are two official routes, and both use the same record. On the 8171 web portal, enter your 13-digit CNIC, complete the captcha step, and submit to see your result. By SMS, send your 13-digit CNIC with no dashes and no extra words to 8171 and wait for the reply. The step-by-step guide to a PMT Score Check explains both routes in detail, including what each possible reply means.

Before you send anything, double-check every digit of your CNIC. A single wrong digit is one of the commonest reasons for a "record not found" message.

What to do if your record is wrong

Do not pay anyone who promises to fix your score for a fee — no agent can change a score from outside the official system. If your household details have changed since the last survey — a new job, a move, a change in family size — the record may simply be out of date. This PMT Score Check Sindh guide walks through how the check works in the province and what to verify first. The fix itself is official only: visit your nearest BISP office in Sindh, ask for a household re-survey, and take your CNIC and supporting documents with you. Once the survey is updated, the score can be recalculated.

Mistakes to avoid

Most problems come down to a handful of avoidable errors: typing the CNIC wrong, sending the SMS to a number other than 8171, or trusting someone who charges money to raise a score. Use only the 8171 portal, SMS to 8171, or a BISP office. And check your score before you need the support, not after you have already been turned away.
